Support

Questions about Voltsy or Volt? Start here, then email us anytime.

Why isn't my battery health exact?

Voltsy uses only Apple's public battery APIs, which don't expose your real battery health percentage or cycle count. Every figure Volt shows is an honest on-device estimate. For the official number, open Settings → Battery → Battery Health & Charging.

How do I get rid of ads?

Voltsy Pro is a one-time purchase that removes all ads and consent prompts forever. Tap "Go Pro" in the app; the App Store shows the price for your country.

I bought Pro but it's gone after reinstalling

Open the paywall and tap Restore Purchases. As long as you're signed in with the same Apple ID, Pro will be restored at no extra cost.

What is the streak / care score?

Keeping Volt in a healthy charge range (roughly 20% to 80%, avoiding heat and deep drains) builds a daily care streak. Days with no data are skipped, so the streak only reflects real charging behaviour.

Does Voltsy collect my data?

No account, no backend. Battery readings stay on your device. The free version shows AdMob ads; see the Privacy Policy for details. Pro shows none.
