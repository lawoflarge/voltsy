# Voltsy Pricing

Voltsy: Battery Care Buddy for iOS (iPhone).

## Free
- Price: $0
- Includes: Volt the battery mascot with live charge reactions, charge alerts, the daily care streak with a free Power Nap freeze, science-based care tips, achievements, a Home Screen widget, and a Lock Screen Live Activity.
- The free version is supported by ads.

## Voltsy Pro
- Price: 4.99 US dollars in the United States, a one-time purchase (non-consumable). No subscription, no recurring charge.
- Unlocks: zero ads forever, full charge history, custom alert thresholds, and unlimited Power Nap streak freezes.

## Notes
- Voltsy is free to download; Voltsy Pro is optional and bought once.
- The App Store shows local-currency equivalents at checkout.
- Source of truth: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/voltsy-battery-care-buddy/id6778094814
