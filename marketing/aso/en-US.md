## Description
Voltsy turns battery care into something you'll actually want to open.

Meet Volt — a cute little battery with a translucent belly that fills up with your charge. Volt reacts live to what your phone is doing: happy and bright in the healthy zone, sleepy when you're running low, dramatic at 1%, and munching away while you charge.

WHY VOLTSY
• A character, not a wall of numbers. Volt makes battery care fun.
• A daily care loop. Keep Volt healthy and build a care streak — one free Power Nap freeze means a single bad day won't reset it.
• Keep it 20–80%. A friendly ring shows how much of your day you spent in the sweet spot.
• Charging Live Activity. Watch Volt's belly fill on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island, with a friendly time-to-80% / time-to-full estimate.
• Home-screen widget. Volt at a glance, in its current mood.
• Achievements. Earn badges for good charging habits.

RADICALLY HONEST
Voltsy never pretends to read Apple's official battery health or cycle count — iOS doesn't allow that, and apps that claim otherwise are guessing. Every Voltsy figure is a clearly-labeled on-device estimate from your own usage, and we even show you how to find Apple's official numbers in Settings.
